## Authority and product fit

Start from captured requirements, accepted specification and the latest submitted
feedback. State the audience, primary task, desired hierarchy, information density,
plain-language conventions and component rules in a short source-bound brief.
Select a reference for those needs, explaining adaptations. A marketing gallery may
inspire restraint and media emphasis without supplying a workbench's navigation,
controls or product requirements. Keep existing behavior and accessibility.

External advice cannot create requirements, permissions, dependencies or approval.
The controller continues to own write scopes, stages, counters and budgets. Apply
changes only as normal structured proposals. When accepted intent conflicts with
the design document, revise the specification/scope in clarify and synchronize
DESIGN.md in prototype. Do not append contradictory promises.

## Critique

Inspect actual screenshots of relevant routes, states and viewports alongside
source and executed interactions. Identify what attracts attention first, whether
that matches the user's main task, and which observed details weaken the intended
experience. Report concrete observations in four dimensions: hierarchy, density,
plain language and consistency. Distinguish an observed requirement violation from
an aesthetic preference. A test pass or detector result alone cannot establish
visual quality. No beauty score or invented threshold determines acceptance.

## Quieter

Reduce competing visual emphasis when secondary actions overpower content.
Consider flatter surfaces, restrained borders, lower secondary contrast and less
decorative motion, while retaining legibility, focus, discoverability and touch
access. Never hide an essential operation merely to make a screenshot cleaner.

## Distill

Remove repetition and decoration that does not support the task. Group related
information and use progressive disclosure where the accepted workflow permits it.
Preserve required information, useful affordances and complete error recovery.
Fewer words or controls are useful only when the intended task remains clear.

## Clarify

Use terms the intended audience understands. Explain what happened and the useful
next action, preserving factual meaning and necessary consequences. Avoid internal
implementation detail, duplicated explanations and speculative promises. A concise
irreversible-action warning may still be necessary. Do not invent progress or
suppress an accepted business rule in the name of simpler copy.

## Polish

Refine the agreed visual direction within scope: spacing, type hierarchy, alignment,
component states, responsive behavior and consistent action treatment. Check the
rendered result again after changes. Preserve the current visual identity and
behavior; a redesign or product decision returns through its owning stage.
Evidence integrity, scope, actions and consent are machine-checkable gates.
Composition and perceived density remain explained model judgments. Advisory
judgments do not block; a defect must bind a real accepted criterion and observation.
